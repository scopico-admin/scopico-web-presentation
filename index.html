Frequently asked questions

What does a fractional CTO actually do?

A fractional CTO gives you senior technology leadership without the full-time hire: technology strategy, roadmaps, build-vs-buy decisions, architecture oversight, and technical credibility — for a fraction of the cost, on the hours you actually need.

How do dedicated development teams work?

You describe the outcome you need; Scopico assembles developers from a vetted network and leads them directly — planning, code review, and delivery included. You get a managed team with senior leadership built in, without the hiring risk.

What does an architecture review cover?

A structured audit of your system's architecture, codebase health, scalability, security posture, and delivery practices — ending with a prioritized findings report and a remediation roadmap, not a vague opinion.

How do you help teams adopt AI-assisted development?

By introducing AI coding tools the way they actually stick: workflow standards, context engineering, code-review guardrails, and team training — led from real delivery experience, so velocity goes up without quality going down.

How do engagements work — availability and payment?

Advisory availability is Monday–Friday 17:30–20:00 CET, plus Saturdays by arrangement; full-time offshore engagements are considered separately. Retainers are billed monthly in advance; fixed-scope work starts with a deposit. Every engagement begins with a paid pilot before any retainer.

What does it cost?

Advisory starts at $120/hour (US) or €110/hour (EU/UK), with day rates, fixed-price architecture reviews from $3,500, and monthly retainers from $2,000. Dedicated teams and full-time engagements are scoped individually — ask for a tailored offer.
