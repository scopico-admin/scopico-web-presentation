About

Scopico is a one-person consultancy with enterprise-scale scars.

Timotej Jakimovski — Founder

13+ years building software, most of it leading teams and owning architecture end to end. Currently development lead on an enterprise platform for Apave France — time, cost, and vehicle-fleet management — built on .NET Core, Angular, and MSSQL on Azure, load-balanced for 15,000 peak users. Leads 6 developers and 2 testers across multinational remote teams.

Earlier work spans banking, insurance, SCADA systems, telecom, and automotive — desktop, web, mobile, and cloud, on Microsoft and Java stacks. Two-time team lead. The common thread: being handed complex, business-critical systems and making them ship.

Why Scopico

Big consultancies sell you a team and bill you for the learning curve. Scopico is the opposite: you work directly with the person who has built the systems, led the teams, and made the hard calls — and who now helps other organizations adopt AI-assisted development without losing engineering rigor.

Based in Skopje, North Macedonia. Working remotely worldwide (CET).

How I work

Selective by design. I take on a small number of engagements at a time, and every client relationship is vetted before committing — on fit, seriousness, and ability to do the work justice.

One client per industry. I never advise two competitors at once. The clients I work with get undivided loyalty and zero conflicts of interest.

Senior only. No hand-offs to juniors, no learning curve billed to you. The person you meet is the person who does the work.
